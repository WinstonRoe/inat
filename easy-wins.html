Speed up large searches with your iNaturalist account

Scoring uses the public iNaturalist API. If you have a lot of pending observations (a few hundred taxa or more), anonymous requests can hit the rate limit. Adding your iNat token raises that ceiling and lets the tool finish in one pass.

Open your API token page (log in to iNaturalist first). Copy the long api_token value and paste it below. Tokens are valid for 24 hours.
